About us

Built on one family's name, run for our clients' benefit.

KR Creaation is a family-owned firm managed by Mr. Sabapathi. Our factory sits in Tirupur — 50 kilometres from Coimbatore airport, and one of India's most important textile cities. Founded in 1999, we specialise in trendy, fashionable knitted outerwear.

Our long-standing clients include established apparel brands and government garment programmes. Every one of our existing clients has worked with us for more than ten years — proof of the co-operation we sustain with the people who trust us with their production.

We currently produce around five crore rupees' worth of garments a year, and we're now actively seeking to grow further into government orders.

Our in-house label
LOXDON

Everything you see here is designed, cut and stitched under our own roof.

LOXDON is KR Creaation's own menswear line — clean silhouettes, honest fabrics and factory-direct pricing, with no middleman between the machine and the label. Since we cut, sew and finish every piece ourselves, we control the fit, the fabric hand-feel and the turnaround on every single run.
